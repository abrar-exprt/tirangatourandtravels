There is no shortage of agencies selling Kashmir. What makes a trip memorable is who is behind it — someone who knows the valley, picks up the phone, and treats your holiday as their reputation. Here is what Tiranga Tour & Travels does differently.

Built by a local: meet Adil Mohammad Lone

Tiranga Tour & Travels was founded by Adil Mohammad Lone, who was born and raised in Pampore — the saffron town on the road from Srinagar to Pahalgam. The agency runs from Galander, Pampore, not from a call centre in another state. When you book with us, the person planning your itinerary knows these roads, hotels and drivers personally.

8 reasons travellers choose Tiranga

1. True local expertise

We plan around real conditions — which roads are open, which valleys are best this week, and the right time to leave for Gulmarg to beat the queue.

2. Itineraries made for you

No copy-paste packages. Your plan is built around your dates, pace, budget and who you are travelling with, whether that is grandparents, young kids or a honeymoon.

3. Transparent pricing, no hidden costs

You get an itemised quote that clearly separates what is included from what is not. What we quote is what you pay.

4. Fast replies on WhatsApp

Most enquiries get a custom itinerary within 2 hours. Fill the form on this page and WhatsApp opens with your details ready to send.

5. 24/7 support during your trip

From airport pickup to departure, you have a direct line to our team at any hour.

6. Comfortable vehicles and experienced drivers

Sedans, SUVs, Innova and Tempo Traveller, driven by local drivers who know mountain roads. See our vehicle options.

7. Hand-picked stays

Hotels, houseboats and homestays we have checked ourselves — chosen for comfort, cleanliness and genuine Kashmiri hospitality. Explore hotel options.

8. Offbeat Kashmir, not just the postcard spots

Beyond Gulmarg and Pahalgam, we arrange trips to Gurez Valley, the Kishanganga river, Habba Khatoon peak and Tulail valley — places many outside agencies do not cover.

Everything in one place

Tour packages, taxi services and hotel bookings all come from the same team, so there is one person accountable for your whole trip. We plan family tours, honeymoon packages, adventure treks, group tours for 10 to 50 people, and fully custom itineraries.
